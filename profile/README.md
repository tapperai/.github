Vantage adds value signals to your bidding. Block removes invalid traffic. Lift finds the leaks in your ad account and fixes them.</p>

## What we build

**Vantage** predicts what each lead or customer will be worth and sends that score to Google, Meta and TikTok. Campaigns then bid for revenue and repeat buyers instead of cheap conversions.

**Block** removes invalid traffic before it costs you money. It connects to your ad accounts and scores every interaction in real time. Invalid clicks, fake leads and bot traffic go onto exclusion lists before they fire a conversion event or waste spend, and real users are never blocked. It covers Search, Performance Max, Meta, TikTok, mobile apps and Display.

**Lift** checks your connected ad account every day and finds where the budget leaks: stalled campaigns, keywords that return nothing, bid targets set out of reach. Each finding shows what it costs you per day in your own currency, so you start with the biggest one. You click Fix and Tapper makes the change, and every change can be undone.

**Screen** gives each lead in Salesforce or HubSpot a label, a score and the reasons, so your team calls real people first.
